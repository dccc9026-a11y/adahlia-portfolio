A little about me…

I'm Adahlia, an integrated marketing professional with over five years of experience connecting organizations with the people they serve. My work has taken me across industries, from public health and life sciences to beauty, consumer brands, and B2B. Along the way, I've worked on everything from brand strategy and campaigns to digital marketing and live experiences.

My relationship with marketing has always lived somewhere between creativity, culture, and understanding what makes people tick.

Outside of marketing, I've established a career as a professional pianist. It's given me a different perspective on storytelling, personal branding, and the power of connecting with an audience.

Creativity has always come naturally to me, but I'm equally interested in the thinking behind it. Understanding an audience, asking the right questions, and seeing how an idea translates into meaningful results are what make the work interesting to me.

Consider this portfolio a collection of the work I'm proud of, the ideas I've brought to life, and a glimpse of what's next.
