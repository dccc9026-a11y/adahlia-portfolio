Scaling an Emerging Haircare Brand Through Content, Community & PR

One founder.

Hairmuse was a one-person business, with its founder managing everything from product development to marketing while balancing a full-time career.

The opportunity was to build a recognizable brand, attract customers, and turn her passion into a sustainable business.
